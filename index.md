Utilities and MCMC implementation for Bayesian inference of multiple
Gaussian graphical models. Supports G-Wishart (Peterson, Stingo, and
Vannucci, 2015, JASA), scalable spike-and-slab (Shaddox et al., 2018,
Stat Biosci), and multi-platform spike-and-slab (Shaddox et al., 2020,
Biostatistics) methods. The multi-platform extension jointly models
networks across multiple data types with a hierarchical MRF coupling
group similarity across platforms. Includes MRF-coupled edge priors for
borrowing strength across groups, parallel multi-chain orchestration,
and helpers to convert precision matrices to partial correlations.
